Australian TV Energy Consumption

Exploring screen sizes, panel technologies, and power standards across the Australian consumer market.

Display Panel Technologies

The screen technology behind modern televisions dictates its power profile:

  • LED/LCD: Generally the most power-efficient option for living rooms.
  • QLED (Quantum Dot): Bright and vibrant with moderate power efficiency.
  • OLED: Pixels light up independently; power consumption scales with screen brightness and HDR content.
3 Types
Major Panel Technologies

LED, QLED, and OLED represent over 95% of televisions sold in Australia.

Screen Size vs Energy Usage

As Australian households upgrade to large displays (65-inch to 85-inch), energy use increases exponentially.

A 75-inch TV often demands up to twice the power of a 50-inch display with the same star rating, making star efficiency crucial for big-screen home cinema setups.

Standby Power Regulations

Under Australian GEMS regulations, television passive standby power is strictly capped between 1 to 2 Watts.

However, smart features like "Networked Standby" and "Instant Wake" can push continuous draw higher if left unmanaged, adding hidden costs over several years.
