The Energy Rating System

Australia’s Equipment Energy Efficiency (E3) Program mandates that major electrical appliances sold in Australia carry the official Energy Rating Label.

The star scale ranges from 1 to 6 stars, with an extended 10-star super-efficiency bracket for advanced technologies. Choosing higher star ratings ensures lower long-term grid demand and continuous energy savings.

Why Star Ratings Matter

Electrical appliances account for roughly 30% of average Australian household energy bills. Selecting higher-rated appliances directly mitigates rising electricity tariffs.

Beyond cost savings, reducing household kilowatt-hours lowers the load on fossil-fuel generation across the National Electricity Market (NEM), directly curbing greenhouse gas emissions.

Annual Energy Consumption (kWh)

Every label prominently prints the appliance's standardized kWh/year usage under average Australian operating patterns.

By taking this figure and multiplying it by your state's current tariff (averaging between $0.30 to $0.35 per kWh), consumers can accurately calculate their exact yearly operating expenses before purchasing.
